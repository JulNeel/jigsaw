# Addendum: Jigsaw

Supplementary depth captured during the brief conversation — parked ideas, mechanic details, and research grounding that inform downstream work (PRD, roadmap) but don't belong in the brief itself.

## Parked Mode Details (post-V1)

**Battle mode**: each player works their own copy of the same puzzle in parallel (not a shared board); power-ups let a player disrupt an opponent's progress, e.g. clearing/scrambling a full row of pieces already placed on the opponent's board. Competitive framing, contrasts with Salon's cooperative framing.

**Time-out mode**: solve against a fixed time limit. Confirmed concept, mechanic details not yet specified (timer length, failure/success state, scoring) — to be defined when this mode is scheduled.

## Parked Feature Ideas (post-V1)

**AI-generated puzzle imagery**: user describes an image, AI generates it, auto-converted to a puzzle. Founder-identified risk: if the generated image reproduces a recognizable licensed character, this is a real IP/legal exposure (unlike incumbents who license character content, e.g. Magic Jigsaw Puzzles' Pixar/Disney/Nickelodeon collections). Needs a resolution path (prompt filtering, legal review, or restricting scope to non-licensed content) before being built, not before being scoped.

**AI-driven difficulty scoring**: AI evaluates an image (generated or user-uploaded) and estimates its puzzle difficulty, as a basis for a more credible difficulty/progression system than the "piece count only" heuristic common in the category.

## Research Grounding (competitive landscape, as of 2026-07)

Captured to ground the brief's problem/differentiation claims; useful as-is for a future PRD or market-research deep dive without needing to re-run this research.

**Incumbents**: Magic Jigsaw Puzzles (Kristanix) — 40,000+ images, licensed collections, subscription $7.99/wk–$99.99/yr. Jigsaw Puzzles Epic — 8,000–20,000+ puzzles, daily free puzzle. Jigsaw Puzzles (Easybrain) — 30,000+ free HD images, "brain exercise" framing. Jigty (Outfit7) — non-repeating piece shapes, custom photo, tournament mode. Jigidi.com / JigsawPlanet.com — free web-native community sites, user-generated content, light monetization, no strong native mobile product.

**Documented UX complaints**: intrusive/unavoidable ads (Magic, Epic); pieces "shoot across the board" or get stuck at screen edges when scrolling; unwanted click/snap sound feedback; UI described as "antiquated"/"trapped in iOS 6"; aggressive subscription upsells with no visible dismiss option.

**Differentiation already attempted by others**: Puzzle Together — real-time multiplayer/co-op with voice chat, 3D physics pieces, AI-description-generated custom puzzles; ~81% positive on Steam (~2,000 reviews), more mixed mobile reviews (wants more content, easier custom-puzzle flow). Jigsaw Together / jigsaw.sh — free, no-signup, browser-based cooperative multiplayer, positioned as social/relaxed vs. competitive. None of these are persistent/asynchronous household products — all are synchronous-session collaborative models, distinct from Salon's "always open, join whenever" model.

**AI-image jigsaw entrants** (emerging, fragmented, no clear leader as of this research): PuzzleFree, Jigsaw Puzzles AI, Gaxos: Jigsaw Puzzle AI, JigsawCat, aijigsawpuzzle.com, NightCafe's jigsaw tool.

**Monetization patterns observed**: ad-supported free-to-play with rewarded video (Epic, Easybrain) is the norm; ad removal via subscription/VIP tier layered on top (Magic, Jigty); one-time IAP puzzle packs (Puzzle Together, ~$8); community sites (Jigidi, JigsawPlanet) monetize lightly, ad-light or ad-free.

**Open mechanic with no dominant player found**: a single Wordle-style "one daily puzzle for everyone" format does not appear to exist yet in this category — noted as a potential future opportunity, not claimed or scoped here.
